A newspaper front page summing up Wednesday, 18 November 2026: a Diplomacy Day.
President Trump meets President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. in Manila, then flies to
Shenzhen for the APEC summit hosted by Xi Jinping, ahead of Thursday's
first-ever US–China–Russia trilateral on nuclear arms control. Around the lead
story: Ukraine hits the Crimea bridge, Iran attacks a US radar in Bahrain,
Taiwan flight rules at the UN, Canada–China trade deals, Israel's opposition
ruling out a joint government, the world court on Myanmar, protests and AI
investment in Manila, and the midterm defeats back home.

The image is drawn entirely by `main.py` — a pure-Python PNG encoder with a
hand-typed 5×7 bitmap font. No libraries, no network, no image assets.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Output: `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (1024×1024, deterministic).
